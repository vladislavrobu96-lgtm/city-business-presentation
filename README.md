# Life in a Big City and Business

A 20-slide presentation on urban life and entrepreneurship. Minimalist design: alternating dark and light slides, one amber accent, one typeface (Manrope), custom bar charts and big-number layouts. Every figure is sourced and dated on the slides.

A self-initiated portfolio project.

**[Open the PDF](Life-in-a-Big-City-and-Business.pdf)**

## Sources

- United Nations, World Urbanization Prospects 2025 (November 2025)
- McKinsey Global Institute, *Urban world: Mapping the economic power of cities*, 2011 (projections to 2025; dated)
- SCORE, based on US Bureau of Labor Statistics data, *Small Business Failure Rates*, 2024 (US data)
- CB Insights, startup failure reasons, as summarized by SCORE
- Popular Science, report on the Centre for Urban Design and Mental Health
- Concepts: Ray Oldenburg's "third place"; Carlos Moreno's "15-minute city"

## Notes

- Mental-health figures are average differences between groups, not predictions for an individual.
- McKinsey figures are from 2011 and are shown as an order of magnitude.
